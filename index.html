dvd.js

Plays a DVD image from an archive.org item, menus included, by streaming the item’s mp4s.

Try:

  • BBS: The Documentary (three discs, still menus, titles split across several VOBs): disc 1, disc 2, disc 3
  • GET LAMP (two discs, an interactive version that asks what to watch next, hidden buttons over the video): disc 1, disc 2
  • Ultimate DVD Demo (a four-angle scene, eight 5.1 language tracks, angle and language menus)
  • Big Buck Bunny (animated menus, extras, 14 title sets)
  • Nina Vision (animated menus, 8 titles)
  • Apollo 16mm onboard select views (no menu; plays from the archive’s own mp4)

Menus: click a button, or use the arrow keys and Enter. Space pauses, ←/→ skip 10 seconds, M opens the disc menu, F is full screen. Titles with more than one audio track, with subtitles or with several camera angles get Audio, Subtitles and Angle buttons under the video (or press A, S and C).

All demos · Source code: github.com/rajbot/dvd.js (GPL-2.0-or-later)
