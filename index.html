dvd.js

Plays a DVD image from an archive.org item, menus included, by streaming the item’s mp4s.

Try:

  • Nina Vision (animated menus, 8 titles)
  • Big Buck Bunny (animated menus, extras, 14 title sets)
  • Apollo 16mm onboard select views (no menu; plays from the archive’s own mp4)

Menus: click a button, or use the arrow keys and Enter. Space pauses, ←/→ skip 10 seconds, M opens the disc menu, F is full screen.
